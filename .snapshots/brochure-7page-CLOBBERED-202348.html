HACK-MATRIX 2026 // ABOUT
HACK-MATRIX 2026About the sprint

Eight hours.
Zero pre-built projects.

Teams receive the challenge at the start of the event and must ideate, design, build, deploy and present a working solution inside that window.

At a glance

When15 October 2026 · 09:00 – 19:00 IST
WhereVVIT Campus, Bengaluru
Entry₹399 per team

Format

  • Build from zero — no pre-built projects, no rehearsed pitches
  • Working demo required — repo, deployment and documentation
  • Nine open tracks — statement revealed live on event day
  • Judged live — shortlisted teams present to the panel
  • Hybrid friendly — remote participation on request

Entry covers

The full build sprint, meals and drinks across the day, on-floor mentor support, and a participation certificate for every team member.

HACK-MATRIX 2026 // PROCESS
HACK-MATRIX 2026How it works

Seven steps, start to finish

From registration to the closing ceremony.

01Register Complete your team registration online.
02Check in Arrive early and complete verification.
03Problem reveal Statements drop at the bell.
04Build Eight hours to make it work.
05Submit Repo, demo and docs before the deadline.
06Demo Shortlisted teams present to judges.
07Results Winners announced at the closing ceremony.

Ground rules

  • Originality — everything is built on event day. Starter code is fine as a foundation, but the core logic must be yours.
  • AI assistance — encouraged. You must be able to defend every part of your solution.
  • Team conduct — respect other teams, mentors and judges throughout.
